1. Installation

  1. Open the Rostrox Anti-Phishing page in the Google Workspace Marketplace and click "Install".
  2. Grant access. The App requests only two permissions: run as a Gmail add-on and view the email while the add-on is running.
  3. Reload Gmail — a shield icon appears in the right-hand side panel.

2. Usage

  • Open an email and click the shield icon. Keep the panel open — every next email is checked automatically.
  • Red bar — dangerous, orange — suspicious, green — no signs of phishing found.
  • Language: close the email (or tap the back arrow) and use "Language" under "Settings" on the home screen.
  • "Why" lists the main reasons; details about the sender, links, text and attachments follow.
  • "Trust" hides minor warnings for a known address; links and attachments are always checked.

3. Error during installation

If you see "Sorry, unable to open the file at this time" after clicking "Install", the usual cause is several Google Accounts signed in to the same browser. Open an incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N), sign in only to the account you want to use the App with, and install it again. After that the App works in your normal window too.

4. No icon in Gmail

Reload Gmail and make sure the App is installed in the same Google Account.

5. "Check failed"

Click "Check again". Until the check succeeds, treat the email with caution.

6. False alarm

Click "Trust" or email rostroxdev@gmail.com. Attach a screenshot of the panel, but please do not forward the suspicious email itself.

7. Uninstall

Google Workspace Marketplace → "Manage apps" → Rostrox Anti-Phishing → "Uninstall". You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
