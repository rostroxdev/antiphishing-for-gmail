Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

In short

  • The app reads only the email you open, and only while its panel is open.
  • Email content is never stored or shared; the app has no internet access.
  • Only your trusted-senders list is stored — in your own Google Account.

1. Data the app can access

Rostrox Anti-Phishing for Gmail™ (the "App"), developed by Rostrox, requests only two Google permissions:

  • gmail.addons.execute — to display the App's panel in Gmail;
  • gmail.addons.current.message.readonly — to read the email you have open while the panel is open.

From the open email the App reads: sender name and address, Reply-To address, subject, body, authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and attachments. It cannot access other emails, contacts or calendar, and cannot send, modify or delete email.

2. How the data is used

Solely to check the open email for signs of phishing and show you the result. The check runs in Google Apps Script within your own Google Account and takes a few seconds. Attachments are never downloaded to your device and never executed.

3. What is stored

  • Email content — never. Once the check is finished, no email data remains anywhere.
  • Trusted senders list. Addresses you mark with "Trust" are stored in Google Apps Script user properties of your account. Only you can see them; Rostrox has no access. Remove them with "Untrust".
  • Your interface language (if you switched it) — also in your own account.
  • Technical error logs — only the error type and code location, with no email content, addresses or text.

4. Sharing

None. The App has no internet access, uses no analytics, advertising or third-party services, and does not sell or share your data. Rostrox cannot see your email.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Your control

  • Uninstall: Google Workspace Marketplace → "Manage apps" → Rostrox Anti-Phishing → "Uninstall".
  • Revoke access: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
