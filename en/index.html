What it checks

Six areas almost every phishing attack relies on.

Sender

A bank name from an unrelated address, look-alike domains such as pr1vatbank, spoofing (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).

Links

Link text that shows one site but leads to another; IP addresses, "@" masking, shorteners.

Email text

Urgency, account-blocking threats, requests for passwords, codes and card details.

Attachments

Real file type, double extensions, macros, login forms, JavaScript in PDFs, archives.

Bank detail changes

"Our bank details have changed" with an IBAN — the scheme that costs businesses most.

Calendar invitations

Invitations with links to third-party sites that calendars add automatically.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and has no ads. You can support development with a donation via the button in the app.

Can you see my email?

No. Checks run within your own Google Account and the app has no internet access.

Does it replace antivirus software?

No. It looks for known signs of phishing and helps you stop in time, but a green verdict is not a guarantee.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, in the Gmail app for Android and iOS — the panel appears below the open email.

Interface language?

English and Ukrainian. Switch on the app home screen (Settings).
